Hi there,

Welcome to ScrumTrip, a free weekly email for fans planning to follow their team to Australia. Every week you'll get key dates, what flights and hotels are doing, and one city guide.

## The big picture
- **24 teams, 6 pools, 52 matches, 7 host cities** (Perth, Adelaide, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Townsville, Newcastle).
- **Pool stage:** 1–17 Oct. **Round of 16:** 23–24 Oct. **Quarter-finals:** 30–31 Oct. **Semis:** 5–6 Nov. **Final:** Sat 13 Nov, Stadium Australia, Sydney.
- **Opening match:** Australia v Hong Kong China, Perth Stadium, Fri 1 Oct.

## Springboks route (Pool B)
Adelaide (3 Oct v Italy) → Brisbane (10 Oct v Georgia) → Perth (17 Oct v Romania). Top the pool and the Boks stay in **Perth** for the Round of 16 on 23 Oct.

## City of the week: Perth
Closest host city to South Africa and the only one with no daylight saving (2 hrs behind Sydney). Trains run straight to Perth Stadium Station on match days. Base yourself in the CBD or East Perth. *(ScrumTrip guidance; check official transport info.)*
